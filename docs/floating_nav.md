# Floating Navigation Bar

A floating navigation bar keeps important links visible as you scroll. This makes it easier to access common actions on long pages.

The bar appears once you scroll past the page header and hides again when you return to the top. It lets you jump to settings or help without losing your place.
The navigation bar now sticks to the top of the page when scrolling.
Adding `position: sticky` to the header keeps links accessible without
wasting space.

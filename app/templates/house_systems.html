How to interpret the checks

Responding means the named TCP port accepted a connection. It does not prove that an automation, calendar sync, camera capture, or delivery succeeded. Unreachable can mean a firewall boundary or a service problem; it does not prove the host is off.

The Sparks are deliberately not probed from Glimpser. Their owning services provide the appropriate health checks. This page does not combine work and personal calendar content or credentials.

Addresses and service placement were reviewed October 1, 2026. This is a curated service map, not automatic physical topology discovery. Older Hubitat presence and temperature tiles may be stale; prefer timestamped evidence over a cached green indicator.

What is it?

Danger Mode reuses your running Chrome instance so Glimpser can capture pages that rely on your logged-in session. Chrome must be started with the --remote-debugging-port={{ danger_info.port }} flag.

How it works

When the debug port is detected and you are idle, an orange icon appears in the navigation bar. Captures marked as "Danger" then open in your browser and close automatically when finished.

Why use it?

Use this mode for dynamic sites that need your real browser context or authenticated session. It allows Glimpser to interact with pages that a headless browser cannot easily access.
